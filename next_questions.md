# Next questions

Ideas that came up during this note but are out of scope for it.

- **Day/night split of the observer → instrument switch effect.** If observers report
  less cloud at night and more convective cloud in summer daytime (CM SAF validation
  report, scenery effect), the switch effect should be larger in winter/at night and
  smaller or negative in summer daytime. Testable with DWD hourly cloudiness (V_N, V_N_I)
  at the 15 switching stations. Came up in Note #1, phase 5 (2026-11-14).
- **Which referee is closer to the truth: observer or ceilometer?** Needs an independent
  reference (e.g. lidar/radar sites, CALIPSO overpasses) at a few stations.
- **Why is CLAAS-3 higher over sea and at Baltic coast/island stations?** Seen in Fig 1
  and Fig 3 of Note #1 (Schönhagen, Greifswalder Oie, Boltenhagen) but not analysed
  (surface type, coastline in the 0.05° cell, viewing geometry).
- **Ceilometer model change (LD 40 → CHM 15k).** 21 of the 36 ceilometer stations used as
  reference in Note #1 changed ceilometer model during 2015–2024. Does the model change
  shift MO_N (same before/after design as the observer → ceilometer test)?
